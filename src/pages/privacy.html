Privacy policy

This page explains what personal information this website collects, why, who handles it, and what you can do about it. We have kept it short because the site collects very little.

Who we are

This website is run by 34 Automation. For data protection law, we are the controller of the information you send us. [PLACEHOLDER: needs Nathan — the legal name of the business, its registered or trading address, its company number if it has one, and the email address for privacy questions]

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we receive your name, your email address and your message. That is all the form asks for. Please don't include anything in your message you would rather we didn't hold.

There are no accounts on this site, no analytics and no tracking. Like any web host, our host keeps short-term server logs of the requests made to the site. These include your IP address, your browser type and the pages you asked for, and they are used to run and protect the service.

How and why we use it

We use what you send through the contact form for one thing: to reply to you and talk about the work you asked about. We don't add you to a mailing list, sell your details or pass them to anyone for marketing.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: you have contacted a business and you would expect a reply. Where your enquiry is about working with us, the basis is also taking steps, at your request, before entering into a contract.

Who handles it

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc., which also receives and stores contact form submissions for us. Netlify acts as our processor, which means it can only use the information on our instructions. Netlify's infrastructure is in the United States. Transfers of UK data to Netlify are covered by the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum in Netlify's data processing agreement.

Netlify checks every form submission for spam using Akismet, a service run by Automattic, Inc. in the United States, as one of Netlify's sub-processors.

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, maps or embedded content. Everything it needs comes from its own address.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • have it corrected if it is wrong
  • have it deleted
  • ask us to limit how we use it
  • object to how we use it
  • receive it in a format you can take elsewhere

To use any of these, email us at the address under "Who we are". We will reply within one month. There is no charge.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first by email at the address under "Who we are". We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome.

You can also complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commission (ICO), at any time:

  • online at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • by phone on 0303 123 1113, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm
  • by post to ICO Head Office, 4th Floor, No.3 Circle Square, 5 Hawkshaw Street, Manchester, M1 7BL
