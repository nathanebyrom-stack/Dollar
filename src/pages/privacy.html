How and why we use it

We use what you send through the contact form for one thing: to reply to you and talk about the work you asked about. We don't add you to a mailing list, sell your details or pass them to anyone for marketing.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: you have contacted a business and you would expect a reply. Where your enquiry is about working with us, the basis is also taking steps, at your request, before entering into a contract. For server logs, the basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

Who handles it

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc., which also receives and stores contact form submissions for us. Netlify acts as our processor, which means it can only use the information on our instructions. Netlify, and the providers it uses to store data (including Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud), are based in the United States, so your information will be transferred outside the UK. These transfers rely on the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum in Netlify's data processing agreement as a fallback.

Netlify checks every form submission for spam using Akismet, a service run by Automattic, Inc. in the United States, as one of Netlify's sub-processors.

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, maps or embedded content. Everything it needs comes from its own address.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • have it corrected if it is wrong
  • have it deleted
  • ask us to limit how we use it
  • object to how we use it
  • receive it in a format you can take elsewhere

Some of these rights apply only in certain circumstances. To use any of them, email us at the address under "Who we are". We will reply within one month. If a request is complex, we may need up to two more months, and we will tell you within the first month. There is normally no charge.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first by email at the address under "Who we are". We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome without undue delay.

You can also complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commission (formerly the Information Commissioner's Office, and still known as the ICO), at any time:

  • online at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • by phone on 0303 123 1113, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm
  • by post to ICO Head Office, 4th Floor, No.3 Circle Square, 5 Hawkshaw Street, Manchester, M1 7BL
