Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information 34 Automation collects, where it comes from, why we use it, who handles it for us, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It covers this website, enquiries, the website scorecard, the emails we send to businesses we would like to work with, and our LinkedIn page.

If we handle personal information on a client's behalf as part of a project, the client's own privacy notice applies to that information, and our contract with the client sets the terms.

When you visit the website

There are no accounts on this site, and it runs no analytics or tracking scripts. When you visit, our host, Netlify, records technical details of each request, such as your IP address, browser details and the page requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. These records are kept for a limited period set by our host. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, maps or embedded content. Images and video are hosted on the site itself. There are no YouTube or LinkedIn embeds.

When you contact us

The contact form has two ways in: "Start a conversation" and "Book a teardown". Both use the same form, and there is no separate booking tool. It asks for:

  • your name
  • your work email address
  • your company
  • your company website (optional)
  • what your enquiry is about
  • any date you are working to (optional)
  • if you are booking a teardown, the times that suit you
  • your message

If you reach the form from a sample page we made for your company (see Sample pages), the form also sends that page's reference, so we know which sample you saw.

Please don't include anything in your message you would rather we didn't hold.

We use what you send to reply to you and to talk about the work you asked about. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: you have contacted a business and you would expect a reply. Where your enquiry is about working with us, the basis is also taking steps, at your request, before entering into a contract.

We never sell your details or pass them to anyone for their own marketing.

The manufacturing website scorecard

The scorecard works out your score inside your browser. Your answers are not sent to us and are not stored, unless you ask for a report.

If you use the share link, your answers are added to the end of the link, after the # sign. Browsers don't send that part of a link to the website, so the answers never reach our server. Anyone you share the link with can see the answers in it.

If you ask for a report, we receive your name, work email address, company, website, show date, score and answers. We use them to prepare and send your report, and to reply if you write back. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: you have asked us for the report.

If we contacted you first

This section is for people we have emailed without being asked. We email you within one month of getting your details, or we delete them by then. That first email says where we got your details and links here.

Who we contact

We research UK manufacturers that are limited companies or limited liability partnerships (LLPs) and that we think could use our help, and we email named people in relevant roles there. We don't email sole traders or ordinary partnerships. The law treats them like private individuals for marketing emails, so they would need to have agreed first.

What we hold and where it came from

We hold your name, your job title, your company, your work email address, a note of where we found your details, and short notes about your company's public website and trade show plans. We get these from public sources:

  • Companies House
  • your company's own website
  • published trade show exhibitor lists

We only use a work email address that you or your company has published. We don't work out addresses from a company's usual format, and we don't use email-checking tools. If no named address has been published, we write to the company's general inbox instead.

Why, and our lawful basis

We use these details to introduce 34 Automation's services to people whose businesses they could help. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: marketing our own services to businesses. UK data protection law names direct marketing as an example of a purpose that can be a legitimate interest. We have weighed this against your interests in a written legitimate interests assessment, and you can ask us about it.

How we email

Nathan checks and sends each email himself, one at a time, from nathan@34automation.uk. That mailbox runs on Google Workspace (see Email). We send you no more than three emails. Every email says who we are and how to reach us.

Your right to object

You can object at any time to us using your details for direct marketing. This right is absolute: if you object, we will stop, and we won't ask you for a reason. To object, reply "no thanks" (or anything that tells us to stop) to any of our emails, or email us at the address under Who we are.

After you opt out

We delete your details except for a short record on our suppression list: your work email address, your company's web domain and the date you opted out. We keep it indefinitely so we never contact you again, including if your details turn up in a future list.

How long we keep it

  • If you don't reply, we delete your details 90 days after our last email to you.
  • If you reply, we treat the conversation as an enquiry and keep it for 12 months, or longer if it becomes work (see How long we keep it).
  • If we collect your details but never email you, we delete them within one month of collecting them.

Sample pages

We sometimes build a sample page for a company we would like to work with. It sits on this site at an address that starts with 34automation.com/m/ and can't be guessed.

  • Sample pages show only information about the company, such as its name, its products and what its own website says. They don't name anyone who works there or show staff photos.
  • They are hidden from search engines and not linked from anywhere on the site.
  • They are taken down 30 days after we create them.
  • They have no tracking or analytics. We don't use them to see who has opened them. Netlify records the same technical details as for any other page (see When you visit the website).

Cookies

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become work: deleted after 12 months.
  • Client records: kept for as long as tax law requires, currently at least 5 years after the 31 January self-assessment deadline for the tax year.
  • Scorecard report requests: deleted 12 months after we send the report, if no conversation follows.
  • Prospects: deleted 90 days after our last email if you don't reply, or within one month of collection if we never email you. If you reply, we keep the conversation as an enquiry. See If we contacted you first.
  • Sample pages: removed 30 days after we create them.
  • Suppression list: work email address, company web domain and opt-out date only, kept indefinitely so that we never contact you again.
  • Netlify server logs: kept for a limited period set by our host.

Form submissions are stored in our Netlify account, and we delete them by hand in line with these periods. You can ask us to delete your information at any time and, unless we need to keep it (for example, for a contract with you), we will delete it from Netlify and anywhere else we hold it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • have it corrected if it is wrong
  • have it deleted
  • ask us to limit how we use it
  • object to how we use it, including an absolute right to object to direct marketing
  • receive it in a format you can take elsewhere
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it

Some of these rights apply only in certain circumstances. To use any of them, email us at the address under Who we are. We will reply within one month. If we need to confirm who you are, or ask what you are looking for, the time limit pauses until you reply. If a request is complex, we may need up to two more months, and we will tell you within the first month. There is normally no charge.
