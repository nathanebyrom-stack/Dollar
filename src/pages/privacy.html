Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information 34 Automation collects, where it comes from, why we use it, who handles it for us, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It covers this website, enquiries, email and our LinkedIn page.

If we handle personal information on a client's behalf as part of a project, the client's own privacy notice applies to that information, and our contract with the client sets the terms.

Who we are

34 Automation is run by Nathan Byrom, trading as 34 Automation. For data protection law, Nathan is the controller of the personal information described here.

For anything about this policy or your information, email nathan@34automation.co.uk. Nathan reads and answers these himself.

When you visit the website

There are no accounts on this site, and it runs no analytics or tracking scripts. When you visit, our host, Netlify, records technical details of each request, such as your IP address, browser details and the page requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. These records are kept for a limited period set by our host. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, maps or embedded content. Images and video are hosted on the site itself. There are no YouTube or LinkedIn embeds.

When you contact us

The contact form asks for your name, your email address and your message. If you email us directly, we receive the same kind of information.

Please don't include anything in your message you would rather we didn't hold.

We use what you send to reply to you and to talk about the work you asked about. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: you have contacted a business and you would expect a reply. Where your enquiry is about working with us, the basis is also taking steps, at your request, before entering into a contract.

We never sell your details or pass them to anyone for their own marketing.

Email

Our mailbox, nathan@34automation.co.uk, is hosted by IONOS, which processes email for us under its data processing terms.

LinkedIn and social media

If you comment on one of our LinkedIn posts or send us a message there, we see what LinkedIn shows us: your name, your profile and what you wrote. We answer on LinkedIn, either directly or through our social media scheduling tool, Buffer (Buffer Inc., United States). We use this only to reply and to continue the conversation, which is in our legitimate interests and yours. If the conversation moves to email, the rest of this policy applies.

LinkedIn is responsible for how it handles your information on its own platform, under its own privacy policy.

AI tools

We use AI writing and research tools to help us draft messages, research companies and prepare documents. A person reads and checks everything before it is sent or used. These tools are provided by companies based in the United States; see Transfers outside the UK.

The information these tools may handle includes:

  • what you send us, such as an enquiry or an email

We don't use AI to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who handles it for us

We use these service providers:

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States) hosts the website and receives and stores contact form submissions, as our processor under its data processing agreement. Netlify checks every form submission for spam using Akismet, a service run by Automattic, Inc. in the United States, as one of Netlify's sub-processors.
  • IONOS hosts our mailbox.
  • Buffer Inc. (United States) is our social media scheduling tool.
  • AI writing and research tools, provided by companies based in the United States (see AI tools).

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing. We would only disclose it to others if the law required us to.

Transfers outside the UK

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States, so your information may be transferred outside the UK:

  • Netlify: the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum in Netlify's data processing agreement as a fallback.
  • Buffer and our AI tools: these providers are based in the United States.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. That is why there is no cookie banner. If that ever changes, this page will change first.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become work: deleted after 12 months.
  • Client records: kept for as long as tax law requires, currently at least 5 years after the 31 January self-assessment deadline for the tax year.
  • Netlify server logs: kept for a limited period set by our host.

Form submissions are stored in our Netlify account, and we delete them by hand in line with these periods. You can ask us to delete your information at any time and, unless we need to keep it (for example, for a contract with you), we will delete it from Netlify and anywhere else we hold it.

Keeping it safe

Only Nathan has access to the accounts where we hold personal information.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • have it corrected if it is wrong
  • have it deleted
  • ask us to limit how we use it
  • object to how we use it, including an absolute right to object to direct marketing
  • receive it in a format you can take elsewhere
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it

Some of these rights apply only in certain circumstances. To use any of them, email us at the address under Who we are. We will reply within one month. If we need to confirm who you are, or ask what you are looking for, the time limit pauses until you reply. If a request is complex, we may need up to two more months, and we will tell you within the first month. There is normally no charge.

Children

This site and our services are for businesses. They are not aimed at anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information about children.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first by email at the address under Who we are. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome without undue delay.

You can also complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commission, at any time. It replaced the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) on 30 September 2026.

  • online at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • by phone on 0303 123 1113, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm
