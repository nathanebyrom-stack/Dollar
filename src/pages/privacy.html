This policy explains what personal information 34 Automation collects, where it comes from, why we use it, who handles it for us, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It covers this website, enquiries, the website scorecard, our newsletter, the emails we send to businesses we would like to work with, and our LinkedIn page.

If we handle personal information on a client's behalf as part of a project, the client's own privacy notice applies to that information, and our contract with the client sets the terms.

  • your company website (optional)

The manufacturing website scorecard

The scorecard works out your score inside your browser. Your answers are not sent to us and are not stored, unless you ask for a report.

If you use the share link, your answers are added to the end of the link, after the # sign. Browsers don't send that part of a link to the website, so the answers never reach our server. Anyone you share the link with can see the answers in it.

If you ask for a report, we receive your name, work email address, company, website, show date, score and answers. We use them to prepare and send your report, and to reply if you write back. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: you have asked us for the report. [CONFIRM: whether Nathan follows up a report request beyond sending the report; if he does, say how]

The form has a separate newsletter box, which is never ticked for you. Asking for a report does not sign you up to the newsletter, and we never treat it as consent to marketing.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • have it corrected if it is wrong
  • have it deleted
  • ask us to limit how we use it
  • object to how we use it, including an absolute right to object to direct marketing
  • receive it in a format you can take elsewhere
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it (for example, for the newsletter)

Some of these rights apply only in certain circumstances. To use any of them, email us at the address under Who we are. We will reply within one month. If we need to confirm who you are, or ask what you are looking for, the time limit pauses until you reply. If a request is complex, we may need up to two more months, and we will tell you within the first month. There is normally no charge.
