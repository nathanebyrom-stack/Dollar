Nathan's story

I started 34 Automation out of a genuine passion for design and creativity. I'd spent years working alongside engineering companies that were, typically, dull and drab — solid businesses hidden behind sites and brands that did them no favours. That gap bothered me enough to build a company around closing it.

How 34 Automation works

We build first. Before a client commits, they've already seen what we can do — that's how we earn trust, and it's why there's no pitch theatre here.

The work itself runs in three steps: we audit where your brand is now, we draw out how you see the business evolving with technology over the next four to five years, and then we nail a new brand and carry out the full design overhaul.

One honest caveat: automation is worth it when it frees your time and keeps your message consistent. If a business is winding down at the end of a career, we'd tell you not to bother — and we'd mean it.
